# Bike-Share FAQ

## Getting Started

**How do I rent a bike?**
Download our app and create an account. Then find a nearby station, tap "Unlock," and scan the QR code on the bike. You can also unlock bikes at station kiosks using a credit card.

**How much does it cost?**
- **Single ride:** $1 to unlock, plus $0.15 per minute
- **Day pass:** $12, with unlimited 30-minute rides for 24 hours
- **Monthly membership:** $20, with unlimited 45-minute rides
- **Annual membership:** $150, with unlimited 45-minute rides

Discounted memberships are available for students, seniors, and low-income residents. Visit our website to apply.

## Using the Service

**How long can I keep a bike?**
Ride as long as you like, but time beyond your plan's included minutes is billed at $0.15 per minute. For longer trips, dock the bike and check out another to reset your time.

**Where can I return my bike?**
Return it to any station with an open dock. Push the front wheel firmly into the dock until the light turns green. If a station is full, use the app to find the nearest one with space, and you'll receive a free 10-minute extension.

**Do I need to bring a helmet?**
Helmets aren't provided, but we strongly recommend wearing one. Riders under 16 are required by law to wear a helmet.

**Are electric bikes available?**
Yes. E-bikes are marked with a green stripe and cost an additional $0.10 per minute. They assist you up to 20 mph.

## Safety and Problems

**What if a bike is damaged?**
Check the brakes, tires, and seat before riding. If something seems wrong, press the red "Report" button on the bike's screen or use the app. Then return it to a dock to take it out of service. You won't be charged for rides under one minute.

**What if the bike is lost or stolen?**
Lock the bike with the built-in cable lock whenever you pause. Use the "Hold" feature in the app for short stops. If a bike is lost or stolen while in your care, report it within 24 hours. Unreported losses may result in a $250 fee.

**Can I ride at night?**
Yes. Our bikes have built-in front and rear lights that activate automatically.

## Account Help

**Can I share my membership?**
No. Memberships are personal, and each account can only unlock one bike at a time. Group rides require multiple accounts.

**How do I cancel my membership?**
Go to Settings > Membership in the app and select "Cancel." Monthly plans end at the close of the billing period, and annual plans can be refunded within 14 days.

**Who do I contact for help?**
Call 555-0142, email help@citybikeshare.example, or use the in-app chat. Support is available daily from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m.
